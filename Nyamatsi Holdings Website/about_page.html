About Nyamatsi Holdings

Nyamatsi Holdings is a dynamic and professional cleaning company dedicated to delivering top-tier cleaning services with reliability, trust, and excellence. Founded with a vision to transform everyday spaces into sparkling clean environments, our mission is to offer both residential and commercial clients a cleaner, healthier space to live and work in.

We take pride in our trained staff, eco-friendly cleaning solutions, and attention to detail that ensures every corner is spotless. From one-time deep cleans to ongoing cleaning contracts, our team is ready to exceed expectations.

Our services include but are not limited to: home cleaning, office sanitation, post-construction cleanup, event cleanup, and customized packages based on client needs.

At Nyamatsi Holdings, we believe in building long-term relationships with our clients, grounded in satisfaction, consistency, and trust. Your space is our priority.
